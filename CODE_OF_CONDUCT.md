# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers of Unity Loom pledge to make
participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this project:

- Being precise about facts, and citing a source for a claim about a version
  of Unity or a package.
- Reviewing a change on its merits, and saying plainly what is missing.
- Accepting that a decision was already settled, and reading the decision
  record before arguing with it.
- Assuming the other person is trying to help.

Behaviour that does not:

- Harassment, in public or in private, including insults, intimidation,
  discrimination, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit
  permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberately derailing a review.
- Dismissing or attacking a person for the level of experience they have.
- Sending unsolicited advertising, or using the issue tracker to promote a
  product.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions — and when an individual is officially representing the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer,
[@SparshKanodia](https://github.com/SparshKanodia), by opening a private
message on GitHub. If the report is about a security problem rather than a
person, use the private vulnerability report channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead — it is faster and it leaves a record.

All reports are reviewed. The maintainer will:

1. Acknowledge the report.
2. Determine what happened, privately, and talk to the people involved.
3. Decide on a response that is proportionate to the behaviour.

Responses may include a private correction, a public warning, a temporary
ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the other maintainers.
